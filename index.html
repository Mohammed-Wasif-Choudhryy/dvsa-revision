Section A: Obscure Highway Code Signs & Visual Traps

High-Fail Traps

Practical drivers fail signs because UK road signs follow strict syntactic rules that are easy to confuse under exam pressure. Memorise the core grammar: Red Circle = Must Not (Prohibition), Blue Circle = Must Do (Mandatory Command), Triangle = Warning (Hazard Ahead), Rectangle = Information / Direction.

Kerb & Road Markings Master Decoder: The Lines Practical Drivers Get Wrong

Single Yellow Line

No waiting during the operational hours displayed on the accompanying roadside time plate. Parking permitted outside those hours.

Double Yellow Lines

No waiting at ANY time. No sign required. You may stop briefly only to set down/pick up passengers unless loading restrictions apply.

Single Kerb Loading Blip

No loading or unloading during the times shown on the accompanying white plate. Check plate carefully.

Double Kerb Loading Blips

Strictly NO loading or unloading at ANY time. Even commercial delivery vans cannot stop.

BOX JUNCTIONS (Rule 174)

Do not enter the box unless your exit road is clear. THE EXCEPTION: You may enter and wait if turning right and only prevented by oncoming traffic.

WHITE ZIG-ZAGS (Rule 191)

Painted approaching Zebra, Pelican, Puffin & Toucan crossings. Strictly NO parking and NO overtaking the lead moving or stationary vehicle.

HATCHED CHEVRONS (Rule 130)

If bordered by a broken white line: do not enter unless safe. If bordered by a solid white line: you MUST NOT enter except in an emergency.

Section B: Vehicle Mechanics, Towing & Safety Systems

High-Yield Technical
⚠️ DPF & Catalytic Converter

DPF Regeneration: Diesel Particulate Filters trap soot. When the DPF warning light illuminates, drive at sustained high speed (40–50+ mph) on a dual carriageway/motorway for 20–30 minutes to generate exhaust heat.

PROHIBITION: Never push-start or tow-start a petrol car with a catalytic converter. Unburnt fuel enters the catalyst, ignites, and permanently melts the ceramic core.

🚐 Caravan Snaking & Cables

Snaking Physics: Caused by crosswinds, passing HGVs, or incorrect noseweight. Counteraction: Ease gently off the accelerator and hold the steering straight. NEVER brake hard or counter-steer, which causes immediate jackknifing.

Breakaway Cable: Thin steel cable clipped between trailer brake and towbar. If trailer detaches, it pulls the trailer handbrake ON before snapping.

Max trailer width: 2.55m | Max length: 7.0m

🛞 Tyres, Blowouts & Brakes

Legal Tread: 1.6 mm across the central 3/4 breadth and around the entire circumference. Check pressures when COLD (driving heats air, giving false high readings). Increase pressures for heavy loads or high-speed driving.

Front Blowout: Car pulls violently to the side of the burst tyre. Grip wheel firmly, ease off gas. Rear Blowout: Rear end fishtails.

Brake Fade: Overheating brake pads on steep downhill descents. Remedy: shift down to a lower gear to utilise engine braking.

Section C: Emergency Protocols, First Aid & Breakdowns

Exam Mandatory
DR ABC Sequence
  • D - Danger: Check for safe approach first.
  • R - Response: Shake shoulders, ask loudly.
  • A - Airway: Tilt head back, lift chin.
  • B - Breathing: Look/listen for up to 10s.
  • C - Circulation: Control severe bleeding.
CPR Exact Numbers

Adult Depth: 5 to 6 cm compressions.

Compression Rate: 100 to 120 per minute (rhythm of 'Stayin Alive').

Ratio: 30 compressions to 2 rescue breaths (or hands-only CPR).

Burns & Helmets

Burns: Cool with clean, cold water for AT LEAST 20 MINUTES. Never remove clothing stuck to burns.

Motorcyclist Helmet: NEVER REMOVE unless airway is blocked or they are not breathing.

Warning Triangle & SOS

Triangle: Place at least 45 metres (147 ft) behind vehicle. NEVER ON A MOTORWAY.

Smart Motorway SOS: Emergency areas every 1.5 miles. Face oncoming traffic when using phone. Walk up verge behind barrier.

🚆 Level Crossing Breakdown: The Strict 1-2-3 Rule
Step 1: Immediate Evacuation Get all passengers OUT of the vehicle and completely clear of the tracks immediately.
Step 2: Railway Telephone Use the dedicated railway phone next to the barrier to alert the signalman.
Step 3: Move Only If Told Only attempt to move the car if instructed by the signalman, and ensure you close gates behind you.

📄 Key Statutory Deadlines & Rules

New Drivers Act (Probation): 6 penalty points within 2 years of passing practical test = Automatic licence revocation. Must retake BOTH theory and practical tests.

Collision Reporting: Must report to police in person as soon as practicable and within 24 HOURS if insurance/contact details were not exchanged.

HO/RT1 Document Production: 7 DAYS to present licence, insurance, or MOT at a chosen police station if requested.

MOT & SORN: First MOT at 3 years old, then annually. SORN (Statutory Off Road Notification) is valid INDEFINITELY until taxed or scrapped.

Horn Curfew: Illegal between 11:30 pm and 7:00 am in built-up areas.

Night Parking: On roads >30 mph, you MUST leave parking lights (sidelights) on and park facing the direction of traffic on the left.

Lab 4: Pedestrian Crossings & Light Cycles Comparison

1. Pelican Crossing

Pedestrian Light Controlled. Features a FLASHING AMBER phase.

Rule: Give way to pedestrians on crossing. If clear, you may proceed.
2. Puffin Crossing

User-Friendly Intelligent. NO FLASHING AMBER. Infrared heat sensors on top.

Rule: Standard light sequence. Lights only change when pedestrians finish crossing.
3. Toucan Crossing

"Two-Can" Cross: Pedestrians AND Cyclists cross together.

Rule: Cyclists do NOT have to dismount. No flashing amber.
4. Pegasus Crossing

For Horse Riders & Equestrians.

Rule: Push buttons are mounted higher up for mounted riders.
5. Zebra Crossing

Flashing yellow Belisha beacons on black/white striped poles.

Rule: MUST give way once a pedestrian steps onto the crossing. White zig-zags forbid parking/overtaking.

4. Statutory Limits, Alcohol & Documentation Thresholds

Tyre Tread Depth:

1.6 mm across the central three-quarters of the tread width and around the entire continuous circumference.

Alcohol Limits:

England/Wales: 35 mcg breath / 80 mg blood / 107 mg urine. Scotland: 22 mcg breath / 50 mg blood / 67 mg urine.

Collision Reporting:

Within 24 hours in person to police if insurance/contact details not exchanged at the scene.

HO/RT1 Document Check:

Must produce licence, MOT, or insurance certificate within 7 days at a nominated police station.

New Drivers Act (Probation):

6 or more penalty points within 2 years of passing practical test = automatic revocation.

First MOT & SORN:

First MOT at 3 years old. SORN declaration remains valid indefinitely.

Horn Curfew:

Illegal between 11:30 pm and 7:00 am in built-up areas, or when stationary.

CPR Compressions:

Depth 5 to 6 cm at rate of 100 to 120 per minute. Burns cooled for 20 minutes.
